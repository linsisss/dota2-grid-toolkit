# Asset sources

The ID 127 target uses `assets/portraits/127.png`, the unmodified Dota 2 screenshot supplied by the user on 2026-09-28. The hero-card renderer displays the portrait area above the screenshot's nameplate (`portraitCrop` in the generated hero data). The model and artwork belong to Valve, outside this repository's MIT license. It is a special Agility entry added first by the data build script. The earlier geometric placeholder `127.svg` is no longer used by the current hero catalog.

Attribute icons in `assets/attributes/` are Valve's original Strength, Agility, Intelligence and Universal icons from `https://cdn.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/apps/dota2/images/dota_react/icons/hero_{strength,agility,intelligence,universal}.png`, retrieved on 2026-09-26. They are served locally and remain Valve's artwork, outside this repository's MIT license.

Hero names, IDs, attributes and roles are an offline snapshot of [OpenDota dotaconstants](https://github.com/odota/dotaconstants/blob/master/build/heroes.json), retrieved on 2026-09-26. The unmodified snapshot is in `data/heroes-source.json`.

Hero portraits in `assets/heroes/` are Dota 2 assets from Valve's public CDN (`https://cdn.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/apps/dota2/images/dota_react/heroes/{name}.png`). Dota 2 and its characters and artwork belong to Valve Corporation. These third-party assets are not covered by this repository's MIT license. This is an independent community editor, not an official Valve product.

Vertical hero portraits in `assets/portraits/{id}.webp` are the in-game hero selection art (`panorama/images/heroes/selection/npc_dota_hero_*.png`), taken from the Spectral.gg Resources Hub (`https://courier.spectral.gg/images/dota/portraits_vert_lg/{name}.png`, 284 × 376, by leamare), retrieved on 2026-09-29 and served locally, never hotlinked. They replace Valve's legacy `_vert.jpg` catalog, which carries outdated art for some heroes, and STRATZ 71 × 94 copies for the newest heroes. Converted with `cwebp -q 85 -m 6 -sharp_yuv`; `node scripts/build-data.cjs` then points the hero data at the WebP files. For a new hero, download its `{name}.png` from the same folder, convert it to `{id}.webp` and rebuild the data. The canvas crops these 71:94 portraits to the tall hero-grid card proportions without stretching them; player cosmetics, hero levels and badges are not included. The artwork belongs to Valve Corporation and is not covered by this repository's MIT license.

Hero-screen backdrops in `assets/backgrounds/` come, since Dota's update of 2026-10-07, from the panorama texture `panorama/images/backgrounds/monster_hoard_2026_background_png.vtex_c` (3840 × 1620, copied from the user's game files on 2026-10-08), blurred by 6 px like every page but the home page and fitted to the user's 1080p screenshot of the «Герои» page with the grid, its cards and glyphs masked (`node scripts/make-grid-background.mjs <png> <screenshot> <out> --blur 6 --content`). Before, they came from the Dota 2 panorama texture `fall_background_png.vtex_c` (…/backgrounds/fall_background.png, 3840 × 2164) supplied by the user on 2026-09-29; its VTEX format 16 stores a plain PNG after the resource blocks. `scripts/make-grid-background.mjs` darkens it the way the game does, fitted to a clean 1080p screenshot of the empty hero grid supplied the same day: `dota-grid.webp` (2×) and `dota-grid-1x.webp` are the part under the 1193 × 593 grid, `dota-screen.webp` the whole 1920 × 1080 screen; lossless WebP, because lossy encoding bands this dark image. The artwork belongs to Valve Corporation and is not covered by this repository's MIT license.

Inter by Rasmus Andersson and the Inter Project Authors (https://github.com/rsms/inter, release v4.1; SIL Open Font License 1.1, `assets/preview-fonts/inter/OFL.txt`) draws the server's link preview pictures (Medium, SemiBold and Bold, unmodified).

Fonts in `assets/dota-fonts/` are downloaded from their authors' releases by `scripts/fetch-dota-fonts.mjs`: Audex, Correction Tape and Divagon by GGBotNet (https://ggbot.net; dafont and itch.io), Monocraft by Idrees Hassan (https://github.com/IdreesInc/Monocraft, release v4.2.1), Bebas Neue by Ryoichi Tsunekawa / Dharma Type (the 2014 family by Fontfabric, with Cyrillic, from https://github.com/dharmatype/Bebas-Neue; Bebas Neue is a trademark of Dharma Type), and from Google Fonts as static instances Comic Relief (Jeff Davis), Cormorant Unicase (Christian Thalmann), Libre Franklin (Impallari Type), Oswald (Vernon Adams and contributors) and Playfair Display (Claus Eggers Sørensen). Each is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the author's license file sits next to the font files as `OFL.txt` and goes into every downloaded archive. Audex, Correction Tape and Divagon are Reserved Font Names: the files in the repository are unmodified, and the customization page renames the fonts inside to Dota's family names only in the archive the user builds for their own game.

The customization page converts menu backgrounds in the browser with [Mediabunny](https://github.com/Vanilagy/mediabunny) by Vanilagy, used unmodified as an npm dependency under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.

Main-menu pieces in `assets/dota-menu/ui/` (top bar, ИГРАТЬ button, the chat line, the Dark Carnival event card, the friends rail's search row, category headers, empty slot, watch button, star, leader crown and party bar) are cut by `scripts/make-menu-sprites.mjs` from a 1920 × 1080 screenshot of the Russian Dota 2 main menu supplied by the user on 2026-09-29; the shards count is painted over, and the user's own avatar and chat picture in the party bar are replaced. They are Valve's interface and artwork and are not covered by this repository's MIT license.

The season-event pieces of the menu preview, `assets/dota-menu/ui/event-*.webp`, are made by `scripts/make-menu-event.mjs` from two 1920 × 1080 screenshots of the Russian Dota 2 menu with GridStudio's event button, supplied by the user on 2026-10-01: the event's logo (lifted off the button's dark ground) and the middle of the «Диковинки Квортеро» event screen; the button's texts are rendered with Valve's Radiance. `assets/dota-menu/ui/profile-preview.webp` (the hover preview of the profile buttons) is drawn by `scripts/make-profile-preview.mjs` with Radiance, the menu preview's avatar and medal; only the Steam icon is taken from the user's 2026-10-01 screenshot of a profile. They are Valve's interface and artwork and are not covered by this repository's MIT license.

The «Герои» page pieces of the menu-background builder, `assets/dota-menu/ui/grid-{subnav,bans,sort,filters}.webp`, are lifted by `scripts/make-grid-page-sprites.mjs` from a 1920 × 1080 screenshot of the Russian Dota 2 «Герои» page supplied by the user on 2026-09-29, against the known background `assets/backgrounds/dota-screen.webp`; the sort box's grid name is painted over. They are Valve's interface and artwork and are not covered by this repository's MIT license.

The logos in `assets/dota-menu/icons/` link the profile buttons to the two sites and remain their owners' marks, not covered by this repository's MIT license: `stratz.svg` is STRATZ's tower mark from the header of stratz.com (retrieved 2026-10-01; the ® removed, placed in a ring like their site icon), `dotabuff.svg` is Dotabuff's white «D» on its red (#ED3B1C), redrawn after the site's `favicon-retina.png`, since the site has no vector logo.

Hero-page pieces in `assets/dota-menu/ui/hero-*.webp` (the preview of the background behind the hero) are made by `scripts/make-hero-page-sprites.mjs` from a 1920 × 1080 screenshot of the Russian Dota 2 hero page (Shadow Fiend, «Снаряжение») supplied by the user on 2026-09-30 and from the same user's export of `pak01_dir.vpk` (`panorama/images/…`: attribute, complexity, role, stat and Aghanim's icons, `control_icons/24px` SVG icons). Only opaque pieces of the screenshot are cut (top bar with the shards count painted over, abilities, item slots, health and mana bars, buttons, the level badge and notes button lifted off by colour); the text is rendered with Valve's Radiance and Reaver fonts, matched to the screenshot. `hero-friends.webp` is the round friends button from that screenshot. `assets/fonts/reaver-{regular,semibold,bold}.otf` come from the user's `game/dota/panorama/fonts/` (Copyright Valve Corporation) and are used by that script only; the site does not serve them. All of it is Valve's interface and artwork, not covered by this repository's MIT license.

The hero in the hero-page preview, `assets/dota-hero/sf-arcana/`, is made by `scripts/make-hero-3d.mjs` from the user's Dota 2 installation (files exported from `pak01` on 2026-09-30): Shadow Fiend's Demon Eater arcana with its head, the Souls Tyrant shoulders, the Arms of Desolation and the arcana pedestal (models with two loadout animations and the «Fiendish Swag!» taunt, material parameters and textures, the fresnel warp texture), the set's particle systems as JSON with their textures, particle snapshots and model attachments, and the hero page's light for Shadow Fiend from `scripts/npc/portraits_full_body_loadout.txt`. Conversion is done by the [Source 2 Viewer](https://github.com/ValveResourceFormat/ValveResourceFormat) command line (release 20.0, MIT). The material follows the formulas of the game's compiled hero shader (`shaders_pc_000.vpk`, `hero_pc_50_ps.vcs`), read with `vkd3d-compiler` and `spirv-cross` (Ubuntu packages); no shader code is shipped. All of it is Valve's game content and is not covered by this repository's MIT license.

`assets/dota-hero/sounds/taunt-fiendish-swag.mp3` is the sound of Shadow Fiend's taunt «Fiendish Swag!» from Dota 2, downloaded on 2026-09-30 at the user's request from the Dota 2 Wiki (`https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/dota2_gamepedia/images/4/46/Taunt_Shadow_Fiend_Fiendish_Swag.mp3`), unmodified; the page plays it at 12 % volume. It is Valve's game audio and is not covered by this repository's MIT license.

## FIGlet fonts («Текст в ASCII»)

`scripts/text-art.mjs` uses [figlet.js](https://github.com/patorjk/figlet.js) (MIT, © Patrick Gillespie and contributors) and the FIGlet fonts it ships (Standard, Slant, Big, Doom, Banner, Graceful, Star Wars, Epic, Alligator2, Larry 3D, 3D-ASCII, Graffiti, Big Money-ne, Merlin1, Fire Font-k, Modular, Rounded, Speed, Ogre, Fender, Shadow, Small, Small Slant, Small Shadow, Rectangles, Cyberlarge), made by FIGlet's authors and contributors; their headers keep their credits. The fonts are loaded only when the «Текст в ASCII» window opens. The «Жирные точки» style follows the lettering of the workshop grid «Seijūrō Akashi» by anesthesia (the technique, not its dots).

The grid installation video guide, `assets/guides/install-grid.mp4` (and its poster `install-grid.webp`, a frame of it), was recorded and voiced by the GridStudio team and supplied by the user on 2026-10-02; it is re-encoded for the web (1080p, 30 fps). It shows the Steam client and Dota 2, which are Valve's interface and artwork and are not covered by this repository's MIT license.

The hero meta in the editor («По мете», «Упорядочить по мете») comes from the [STRATZ API](https://stratz.com/api) — ranked All Pick win and match counts per position — and is credited «Powered by STRATZ» with a link to stratz.com wherever it is shown, as STRATZ asks. GridStudio's server queries it with its own key; no STRATZ data is stored in this repository.

The rank medals `assets/ranks/rank1…8.webp` (Herald to Immortal) and the position icons `assets/ranks/position1…5.webp` of the editor's meta window come from the user's Dota 2 files (`panorama/images/rank_tier_icons/rank*_psd`, `…/handicap/*icon_psd`, exported 2026-09-29), scaled down. They are Valve's interface and artwork and are not covered by this repository's MIT license.

## Dota 2 HUD and main menu for the interface builder (2026-10-16)

`assets/dota-hud/panorama/images/**` and `assets/dota-menu-ui/panorama/images/**` are Valve's own Panorama textures from the game files (`pak01_dir.vpk`, exported with Source 2 Viewer, converted to WebP; the HUD lossless, the menu's large ones at quality 90), `assets/dota-hud/panorama/{layout,styles}/**` Valve's Panorama layouts and styles as Source 2 Viewer decompiles them (also published by spirit-bear-productions/dota_vpk_updates), `assets/dota-hud/world.webp` a screenshot of the game world without the HUD. They draw the preview of «Интерфейс» (`src/customize/panorama/`) the way the game does, for a mod of the user's own game. © Valve Corporation; Dota 2 is a trademark of Valve. `assets/dota-hud/preview/*.webp` (the hero's 3D portrait with its stats, the minimap, the hero in the top bar, the innate ability's icon and the two icons over the inventory, which Dota draws without Panorama textures) are cut by `scripts/make-hud-preview.mjs` from the user's 1920 × 1080 screenshots of Keeper of the Light in demo mode (2026-10-16). This preview also loads Valve's `assets/fonts/radiance-{light,regular,semibold,bold}.otf` and `reaver-{regular,semibold,bold}.otf` (from the user's `game/dota/panorama/fonts/`) under its own font names, in its own lazily loaded chunk, so its texts are drawn with the game's fonts; no other page of the site serves them.

Valve's cursor pictures in `assets/dota-cursors/<id>.png` (the cursor creator's icons and faded guide) are made by `scripts/make-cursor-guides.mjs` from Dota 2's own `resource/cursor` BMPs, as shipped unchanged in the «Default Cursor» pack of Dota2PornFX (https://github.com/h6rd/Dota2PornFxWeb, `assets/files/cursors/Default Cursor.zip`, retrieved 2026-10-17): the largest picture up to 96 px of each id, converted to PNG. They are Valve's artwork and are not covered by this repository's license.

The «d2pfx» cursor packs on the customization page are not stored in this repository or on GridStudio's servers: the page reads Dota2PornFX's index (`assets/data/mods.json`, `constants.json`), previews and pack ZIPs directly from h6rd/Dota2PornFxWeb on GitHub in the user's browser, and shows each pack with its author as Dota2PornFX credits it (or «h6rd / Dota2PornFx» when no author is listed, as its README asks), also in the built archive's read-me. Dota2PornFX's own code and mods are GPL-3.0 with attribution terms; other authors' mods and Valve's files stay with their owners (its ATTRIBUTION.md).

The other d2pfx mods of the customization page (HUD skins, the menu's and the HUD's parts, click colours, pings, creep denies, the voice icon; `scripts/d2pfx-mods.mjs`) are not stored here either: `src/customize/d2pfx-mods.json` lists them (names, d2pfx's preview addresses, credits, sizes) and `server/d2pfx-files.json` holds only the paths and SHA-256 of their files, both made by `scripts/make-d2pfx-catalog.mjs` from h6rd/Dota2PornFxWeb at one commit; the browser downloads the chosen mods from GitHub at that commit. The exception is `assets/d2pfx-click/<mod>/`: the click mods' particle definitions decompiled with Source 2 Viewer into the simulation's JSON, and Black Click's own textures (downscaled to 256 px), made by `scripts/make-d2pfx-click.mjs` so the match preview plays each mod's real effect. They belong to the mods' authors as credited in each `fx.json` and on the page (Purple and Blue Click: NahuiToSay; White Click: 3atvorn1k; Light Brown and Sapphire Click: YuyaKizami; Black Click: Morecursed — via Dota2PornFX), are derived from Valve's particle files, and are not covered by this repository's license.

`assets/d2pfx-hud/` holds layers rendered by `scripts/make-d2pfx-hud.mjs` for the HUD-skin and HUD-part preview: Dota's HUD laid out by our Panorama emulator from Valve's files (`valve/`) and with each d2pfx HUD skin's or part's own textures, stylesheets and layouts (`<mod>/`, only the pixels the mod changes; decompiled with Source 2 Viewer from the mod's VPK), plus thumbnails of the captured HUD with each mod. The mods belong to their authors as credited on the page (via Dota2PornFX); Valve's files are Valve's. Not covered by this repository's license.

The click feedback textures in `assets/dota-click/` (arrow_down, arrow_down_02, arrow_down_outline, arrow_down_outline_02, arrow_down_shadow, lens_flare sequence 9, particle_glow_04, particle_square_gradient_2) and the particle definitions in `src/customize/click-fx.json` are decompiled with Source 2 Viewer (20.0) from Dota 2's own `materials/particle/` and `particles/ui_mouseactions/clicked_occlusion_*` files (the dota-loadout depot cache, 2026-10-17), the textures downscaled to 128 px. They are Valve's game content and are not covered by this repository's license.
